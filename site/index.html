02 / Use

Keep the URL.
Move the handler.

No provider reconfiguration. No temporary callback URL. When you stop the claim, traffic returns to the original app.

  1. 01

    Start your service

    Run the webhook handler on your laptop, for example on port 8080.

  2. 02

    Claim its path

    tul claim '/webhooks/stripe/*' -p 8080
  3. 03

    Debug live traffic

    Requests appear in the terminal and reach localhost. Press Ctrl + C to release.
